# Sponsor-Safe Language

Use this language in demos, reports, and advisor conversations.

## Preferred phrasing

Use:

- likely issue
- exception detected
- requires review
- estimated impact
- source-data variance
- evidence packet
- recommended review step
- coordinate with TPA, recordkeeper, payroll, auditor, or counsel as needed

Avoid:

- violation confirmed
- fiduciary breach
- illegal
- guaranteed correction amount
- legally required fix
- compliant / noncompliant as a final conclusion

## Report framing

Preferred:

> "ProofLink detected a compensation/match exception that should be reviewed against the plan document, payroll configuration, and recordkeeper postings. Estimated dollar impact is based on the rules and source data provided."

Avoid:

> "ProofLink determined the plan is out of compliance."

## Advisor positioning

Preferred:

> "This gives us an operational risk scan and evidence packet. It does not replace the TPA, auditor, or ERISA counsel. It helps everyone find the issue faster and work from the same source data."

## Sponsor positioning

Preferred:

> "This is designed to catch payroll-driven retirement plan issues before audit season or participant correction work becomes more expensive."

## Correction language

Preferred:

> "Review with the TPA/recordkeeper to confirm whether corrective contribution, reclassification, payroll mapping change, or other action is appropriate."

Avoid:

> "The sponsor must make this exact correction."
